About

A quick, private second opinion on your writing and your numbers.

Tajzia checks CVs, assignments, research reports, theses, and financial statements against plain, well-known rules — instantly, in your browser, with no AI and nothing uploaded to a server.

What Tajzia does

Document analysis

Paste or upload a CV, assignment, research report or thesis. Tajzia checks structure, length, clarity, citations and evidence, and gives an overall score with specific fixes.

Financial analysis

Enter — or upload — an income statement and balance sheet. Tajzia calculates standard liquidity, profitability, leverage, efficiency and growth ratios, plus a plain-language summary of what they mean.

How it works

Tajzia does not use AI, language models or any external analysis service. Every check is a fixed rule or a standard formula:

  • Document checks look at things like word count, sentence length, readability, passive voice, section headings, citation counts and repeated keywords.
  • Financial checks use textbook formulas — current ratio, gross and net margin, debt-to-equity, ROE, days sales outstanding, and so on — applied to the numbers you provide.
  • Everything runs as JavaScript in your own browser. Nothing you type or upload is sent to a server or stored anywhere.
Because it is rule-based, Tajzia is consistent and transparent — but it is not judgment. It can miss context a careful human reader would catch, and it should be treated as a first pass, not a final word.

What Tajzia is not

  • Not a grade or a verdict. The score reflects structure and language patterns, not the quality of your ideas or arguments.
  • Not a plagiarism checker. Tajzia cannot tell you whether text was copied from somewhere else.
  • Not financial or legal advice. Ratio benchmarks are general rules of thumb — always compare against your own industry, and consult a qualified accountant or advisor before making decisions.
  • Not a live data source. Currency exchange rates are approximate defaults that you can edit; they are not pulled from a live feed.

Privacy

Tajzia runs entirely client-side. There is no account, no login and no upload endpoint — the text and figures you enter stay on your own device for the length of your session. Reloading the page clears everything.

Languages & currency

The interface is available in English, Urdu and Sindhi, and figures can be displayed in PKR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, CNY or INR using rates you can adjust yourself. English-language checks such as readability and passive-voice detection are most reliable on English text.
